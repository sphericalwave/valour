# Valour — Business Proposal

**A branded, self-owned tournament platform for your jiu-jitsu events.**

*A working demo is live at https://sphericalwave.com/valour/ — themed with your Valour Series branding. This isn't a mockup; it's the actual software, running.*

---

## The opportunity

Every tournament you run on a third-party platform pays a recurring toll — a per-athlete service fee on every registration, on every event, indefinitely — and in exchange you get a platform that carries *their* brand, holds *your* data, and only does what *they* decide to build.

You have the events, the athletes, and the brand. What you don't own is the software. This proposal fixes that: **your own platform, your colours, your domain, your data — with the per-athlete toll removed.**

## What's already built

The demo already does the full event lifecycle — this is real, deployed software, not slides:

- **Registration & entry-fee checkout** for competitors
- **Live brackets** (single-elimination) that update in real time as matches are scored
- **Referee scoreboard** — points (2/3/4), advantages, penalties, running clock, submission/decision — that auto-advances winners through the bracket
- **Admin bracket control** — generate brackets from entrants (with byes), declare winners, edit
- **Staff & referee payroll** — roster your crew, set per-event / hourly / per-match rates, and pay them out in a batch
- **Multiple tournaments** under one brand — run, switch between, and clone events
- **Organizer dashboard** — registrations, revenue, win methods, medals by team
- **Cloud-backed** — data persists and syncs live across devices (Supabase)

Remaining before running real money: connecting live **Stripe** for charges and payouts, and turning on production security — both are well-understood, green-lit once you're in.

## What it costs *you* to run

Once built, your only variable cost per event is standard payment processing (Stripe, ~2.9% + 30¢) — the same anyone pays anywhere. Hosting is effectively free to a few dollars a month regardless of how many athletes register. **No per-athlete platform fee.**

| Registrations / year | Typical SaaS service fees | Your cost on Valour |
|---|---|---|
| 500 | $1,500 – $2,500 | ~$0 – $300 |
| 1,500 | $4,500 – $7,500 | ~$0 – $300 |
| 3,000 | $9,000 – $15,000 | ~$0 – $300 |

Those savings recur **every year**.
